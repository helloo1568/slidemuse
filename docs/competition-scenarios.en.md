# Competition presentation scenarios

[← Back to home](../README.en.md)


Use project proposals, research papers, survey reports, and the current competition brief as source material. The examples below suggest ways to organize a presentation; confirm the outline against the requirements of your track. The briefs below were checked on 2026-11-01; always re-confirm against the current season's official notice.
